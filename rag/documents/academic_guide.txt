Academic Performance Guide

Attendance Guidelines

Attendance above 85% is considered good.
Attendance between 75% and 85% is acceptable, but students should try to improve their attendance.
Attendance below 75% needs attention because regular attendance is important for academic performance.

Academic Performance

An average mark of 85 or above generally indicates excellent academic performance.
An average mark between 70 and 84 generally indicates good academic performance.
An average mark between 50 and 69 generally indicates average academic performance.
An average mark below 50 indicates that the student needs significant academic attention.

Study Recommendations

Students should give additional attention to subjects in which they have lower marks.
Students should maintain consistent study habits instead of studying only before examinations.
Reviewing mistakes from previous examinations can help improve future performance.
Students should monitor both their marks and attendance regularly.

Performance Trends

An improving trend means that the student's recent marks are higher than their earlier marks.
A declining trend means that the student's recent marks are lower than their earlier marks.
A stable trend means that there has been little significant change in the student's marks over time.

Academic Risk

Academic risk can be influenced by both marks and attendance.
Low average marks or consistently low attendance may indicate that a student needs additional academic attention.
Students with good marks and good attendance generally have lower academic risk.
